{%- comment -%} Assigns `active_certifications`: entries of _data/certifications.yml shown on the about page. Expired entries (checked at build time), edX courses and entries flagged `hide: true` are left out. Shared by the about page certifications section and its JSON-LD (_includes/jsonld.html). {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign this_month = site.time | date: "%Y-%m" -%} {%- assign active_certifications = "" | split: "" -%} {%- for cert in site.data.certifications.certifications -%} {%- if cert.issuer == "edX" or cert.hide -%}{%- continue -%}{%- endif -%} {%- if cert.expires and cert.expires < this_month -%}{%- continue -%}{%- endif -%} {%- assign active_certifications = active_certifications | push: cert -%} {%- endfor -%}
